                                                     W  H A T I S D I F F E R E N T                                                 
                                                                                                                                    
    •  Single Experience / Access layer through Flywire CRM providing one navigation model, one identity/SSO footprint, and one customer view across all three legacy business lines
                                                                                                                                    
       (Flywire, Sertifi, Invoiced), regardless of which org originally owned the record                                            
    •  Single E2E quote-to-cash workflow with one quoting engine, defined owners at each stage, standardized approval routing, and one process model applied across all three product
       lines instead of three parallel configurations                                                                               
    •  Governed master data model with standardized entity definitions, parent-child hierarchy support across the combined customer base, a single reconciled pricing/product catalog, and
       an active deduplication strategy applied during migration and on an ongoing basis - not just cleanup once                    
    •  Simplified release & operational overhead - One release calendar, one sandbox/testing strategy, and one admin function instead of three independently managed org lifecycles.
    •  Standardized, documented integration architecture consolidating today's point-to-point connections onto one platform (Workato, already in place) with automated end-to-end
       handoffs, no manual re-entry, and reusable patterns across every downstream Finance, Marketing, and Support system           
    •  Unified analytics & reporting layer - One governed data platform (Flywire-only BigQuery) replacing Smartsheet/Excel/Google Sheets patchwork sitting alongside it
    •  Backup, recovery, and business continuity coverage extended to the full consolidated org from day one — not the Flywire-only coverage that exists today
