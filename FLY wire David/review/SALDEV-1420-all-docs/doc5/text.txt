To clarify - the groups should be ordered by Start Date plus Subscription Term when the Start Dates are identical. 
In this way, non-ramped groups that run the entire quote's term will appear on top
Then the ramped groups will appear in sequential order by start date only, not by subscription term
EG:
Groups 1-3 have Subscription Term = 12 and are ramped
Group 4 has Subscription Term = 36 and is not ramped
 
The email template will have group 4 first because it has the same start date as group 1, but the longer subscription term
Then it will have groups 1-3 in that order (ie by start date) because they have shorter subscription terms.
This will also address scenarios where group 1 = 12 months, group 2 = 6 months, group 3 = 18 months.  They should appear in 1, 2, 3 order based on their start dates, not the subscription terms.
