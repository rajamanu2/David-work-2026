                                                                        RECOMMENDED                                                 
                                                                                                                                    
            Data Unification                                           Org Consolidation                                            
                                                                                                                                    
            Harmonize data across the 3 orgs; keep orgs separate       Bring all 3 orgs into one unified platform                   
                                                                                                                                    
            • Custom CRMs remain; they are just reported on together   ✓ One CRM built once; no per-business unit customization     
            • Systems and teams remain operationally disconnected      ✓ Systems and teams operate on a single platform – more easily enables
                                                                                                                                    
            • 360-view is a dashboard, not a real customer record      convergence of process across teams                          
                                                                       ✓ 360-view is the actual, governed customer record           
            • Every future build (ex: CLM) still needs to be done 3 times                                                           
            • Governance, maintenance, and tech debt will continue to grow ✓ Q2C/CLM/AI and other future enhancements are built once
                                                                       ✓ Lower cost of ownership and easier ongoing maintenance     
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                             Consolidation  is the only path that closes all 3 gaps at the platform level.                          
                                      Data unification only masks  them  at the reporting layer.
