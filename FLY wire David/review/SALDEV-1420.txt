P155 [normal]: To clarify - the groups should be ordered by Start Date plus Subscription Term when the Start Dates are identical.
P156 [normal]: In this way, non-ramped groups that run the entire quote's term will appear on top
P157 [normal]: Then the ramped groups will appear in sequential order by start date only, not by subscription term
P158 [normal]: EG:
P159 [normal]: Groups 1-3 have Subscription Term = 12 and are ramped
P160 [normal]: Group 4 has Subscription Term = 36 and is not ramped
P162 [normal]: The email template will have group 4 first because it has the same start date as group 1, but the longer subscription term
P163 [normal]: Then it will have groups 1-3 in that order (ie by start date) because they have shorter subscription terms.
P164 [normal]: This will also address scenarios where group 1 = 12 months, group 2 = 6 months, group 3 = 18 months.  They should appear in 1, 2, 3 order based on their start dates, not the subscription terms.
